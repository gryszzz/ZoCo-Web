See what is happening inside the line.

A camera inspection can help when a drain problem keeps returning, when the condition of an accessible line is uncertain, or when a visual check may clarify what is happening beyond the fixture.

The camera provides a view of visible conditions in accessible portions of the line. It can help locate obvious trouble areas and support a more informed conversation about next steps.

Sussex + Passaic County service.

ZoCo serves residential customers across Sussex County and Passaic County, New Jersey. Call or text with the property location and a short description of the problem.

Common questions

What can a drain camera show?

A camera can show visible conditions in accessible portions of a drain or sewer line, including obvious buildup or trouble areas that are within view.

Is a camera inspection always necessary?

No. Whether it is useful depends on the symptoms, history of the line and access. ZoCo can help determine whether it makes sense for the job.
