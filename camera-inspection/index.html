Common questions

What can a drain camera show?

A camera can show visible conditions in accessible portions of a drain or sewer line, including obvious buildup or trouble areas that are within view.

Is a camera inspection always necessary?

No. Whether it is useful depends on the symptoms, history of the line and access. ZoCo can help determine whether it makes sense for the job.
