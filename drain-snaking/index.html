When a cable may be the right tool.

Drain snaking uses a flexible cable to work through certain clogs and restore flow. It is commonly associated with buildup or obstructions in accessible lines, but not every drain problem should be treated the same way.

ZoCo can evaluate the symptoms and access point before confirming whether cable-based clearing makes sense. If the problem appears to need a different approach or further inspection, the scope can be discussed before scheduling.

Local service, direct to the owner.

ZoCo is owner operated in Highland Lakes and serves Sussex County and Passaic County. When you call or text, include which fixtures are affected, whether the problem is recurring, and the property location.

Common questions

Is drain snaking the same as drain cleaning?

Drain snaking is one method of drain cleaning. The right approach depends on the clog, access point, line condition and symptoms.

Can you tell if a camera inspection is needed?

If the issue is recurring or the line condition is uncertain, camera inspection may be useful. ZoCo can discuss whether it fits the job.
